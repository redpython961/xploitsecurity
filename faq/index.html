General

What is Xploit Security?

Xploit Security is the cybersecurity services and technology arm of the Xploit family, operated by Xploit Cyber Security Services, a company registered in the United Arab Emirates. We provide IT and OT cybersecurity consulting, compliance and assessment services, and we build commercial platforms: SafeMind (awareness SaaS), Xploit GRC (governance, risk and compliance) and the SCADA Simulator (OT awareness and testing).

For cybersecurity courses and certifications, see our sister brand Xploit Academy.

What industries do you support?

We work with critical infrastructure, enterprise and government organizations, including energy and power, water and wastewater, oil and gas, manufacturing, financial services and fintech, telecom and cloud providers, healthcare and pharma, SaaS and technology companies, and defense-industrial-base suppliers.

What geographical markets do you serve?

We're headquartered in Dubai, UAE, with regional presence in the United States, the United Kingdom and India. We have deep experience with UAE and Saudi regulations (NESA, CBUAE, ADGM, DIFC, NCA ECC, NCA CCC, SAMA, Aramco SACS-002) and deliver international standards such as ISO 27001, SOC 2, CMMC, PCI DSS and GDPR for clients worldwide.

Do you provide remote consulting?

Yes. Most advisory, vCISO, compliance and GRC work is delivered remotely, with on-site visits where they add value, such as OT site assessments, workshops or audits. Penetration testing can be performed remotely or on-site depending on scope.

Cybersecurity services

Do you provide vCISO services?

Yes. Our vCISO services give you a senior security leader who owns strategy, governance, risk, board reporting and compliance programs on a flexible, fractional basis, for startups, scale-ups and enterprises.

Can you help organizations achieve ISO 27001 certification?

Yes. We provide end-to-end ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation and certification readiness: scoping, risk assessment, Statement of Applicability, policies and controls, internal audits by certified lead auditors, management review and support during the certification audit. The certificate itself is issued by an accredited certification body.

Do you provide SOC 2 and CMMC consulting?

Yes. We deliver SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2 readiness, control implementation and auditor coordination, and CMMC Level 1 and Level 2 readiness, including NIST SP 800-171 assessments, SSP and POA&M preparation. See Compliance & Risk Management.

Do you perform IT and OT cybersecurity assessments?

Yes. We perform IT security assessments, penetration testing (web, mobile, IoT and networks), cloud security audits for AWS, Azure and GCP, and OT/ICS security assessments aligned with IEC 62443 and NCA OTCC, planned around safety and uptime. See IT Cybersecurity and OT & Industrial Cybersecurity.

Products

What is SafeMind?

SafeMind is a cybersecurity awareness platform that combines 100+ microlearning lessons, unlimited realistic phishing simulations, AI-powered learning journeys, reporting by individual, department or organization, and awareness tips delivered in Microsoft Teams and Slack.

What is Xploit GRC?

Xploit GRC is a cybersecurity governance, risk and compliance platform. It centralizes risk registers, compliance and control assessments, policies, audits, findings and corrective actions, IT and OT assets, third-party risk, evidence and executive dashboards, with a library of 38+ frameworks and the ability to import your own.

What is Xploit Firewall Security Analyzer?

Xploit Firewall Security Analyzer (XFSA) audits firewall configuration exports against a version-controlled rule library. It flags known firmware vulnerabilities (CVEs), over-permissive and risky rules, exposed management, weak VPN cryptography, and logging and inspection gaps. It scores compliance against standards such as NIST SP 800-53, PCI DSS 4.0.1 and vendor hardening guidance, and produces a report with configuration evidence and remediation for every finding. XFSA is available as a cloud SaaS or as a desktop application installed on your own infrastructure. FortiGate is fully supported today; contact us about other vendors.

Can I try your products before buying?

Yes. You can request a free trial of XFSA (SaaS or Desktop), and a guided Proof of Concept (POC) for SafeMind or Xploit GRC. You can evaluate each platform in your own environment before making a purchasing decision.

Can Xploit GRC be hosted on-premises?

Yes. Xploit GRC is available as a tenant-isolated SaaS platform, with optional KSA or UAE data residency and dedicated hosting, and it can also be deployed on-premises in your own environment. Contact sales to discuss on-premises licensing.

Is SafeMind available as SaaS?

Yes. SafeMind is a cloud-native SaaS platform running on AWS with a multi-tenant architecture. Pricing is per user per month on a 12-month commitment, starting at $3.40 for 25 to 50 users and decreasing with volume.

What is the SCADA Simulator?

The SCADA Simulator is a tailor-made OT cyber range with authentic Siemens, ABB, Rockwell or Mitsubishi PLCs connected to Level 0 devices and a miniature replica of your plant. It's used for OT awareness training, testing system changes and rehearsing cyberattacks, with an optional attack-simulation add-on covering the full attack chain.

Are the OT attack scenarios free?

Yes, completely free. The OT Cyber Range offers ten browser-based OT/ICS incident scenarios plus a certification exam. Start the free scenarios.
