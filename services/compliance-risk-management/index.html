Programs in focus

Our most requested compliance engagements.

ISO 27001 implementation

End-to-end ISMS implementation and certification readiness: scope, risk assessment, Statement of Applicability, policies, internal audit and management review.

  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 aligned
  • Internal audits by certified lead auditors
  • Certification audit support

SOC 2 readiness

Readiness assessment, control design and evidence preparation for SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2 attestation, including year-over-year renewals.

  • Trust Services Criteria mapping
  • Policy and control implementation
  • Auditor coordination

CMMC Level 1 & 2

Scoping of FCI and CUI environments, NIST SP 800-171 assessment, POA&M and SSP preparation for defense-industrial-base suppliers.

  • 110 NIST SP 800-171 requirements
  • SSP and POA&M development
  • Readiness reporting
Risk management

Risk you can explain to the board.

Compliance is the floor, not the ceiling. We build cybersecurity risk management that identifies, scores and treats risk transparently, with likelihood × impact scoring, defined appetite, accountable owners and tracked treatment plans.

  • Risk methodology (3×3, 4×4 or 5×5) and appetite statement
  • Asset-based IT and OT risk assessments
  • Treatment plans, risk acceptance and approvals
  • Key risk indicators and executive reporting
