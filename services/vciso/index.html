Virtual CISO

Executive security leadership, without the full-time overhead.

Our vCISO advisory services offer strategic insight to help startups and enterprises navigate complex challenges. You get an experienced security leader embedded in your team, setting direction, making decisions and representing security to leadership, customers and auditors.

It's ideal when you need to answer customer security questionnaires, prepare for SOC 2, ISO 27001 or CMMC, respond to a regulator, or mature a security function ahead of a full-time hire.

Strategy

Security vision, priorities and a roadmap aligned to business goals.

Governance

Policies, committees, risk appetite and accountability.

Reporting

Clear metrics and board-ready briefings on posture and risk.

Assurance

Audits, certifications and customer due diligence handled.

Typical vCISO responsibilities

Everything a CISO owns, delivered on your terms.

Program leadership

  • Security strategy, roadmap and budget planning
  • Information security policies and standards
  • Risk register ownership and treatment decisions
  • Security committee setup and facilitation
  • Vendor and third-party risk oversight

Compliance & assurance

  • ISO 27001, SOC 2 and CMMC program ownership
  • Audit preparation and auditor liaison
  • Customer security questionnaires and trust reviews
  • Incident response planning and tabletop exercises
  • Regulatory alignment: NCA ECC, NESA, SAMA, CBUAE, ADGM, DIFC
