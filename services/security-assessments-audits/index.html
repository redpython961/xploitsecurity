Security assessments & audits

See your gaps before an auditor or attacker does.

Gap assessments, internal audits and security reviews across IT, cloud and OT, delivered by certified auditors with findings tracked through to closure.

Request a Consultation Talk on WhatsApp

Assessment types

  • Gap assessments against any framework
  • ISO 27001 internal audits
  • IT and OT/ICS security assessments
  • Cloud security audits
  • Penetration tests and security reviews
  • Third-party / vendor assessments
Know exactly where you stand

Independent assessments with findings you can act on.

An assessment is only as good as what happens next. Ours end with prioritized, owner-ready findings, not a report that sits on a shelf.

Gap assessments

Measure your current controls against ISO 27001, SOC 2, CMMC, NCA ECC, NESA, IEC 62443 or your own policy set, and size the work to close each gap.

Internal audits

ISO 27001 internal audits conducted by certified lead auditors, with nonconformities, observations and corrective-action follow-up.

Security reviews

Focused reviews of architecture, configurations, identity, logging and incident readiness, in IT, cloud or OT environments.

Our assessment approach

Evidence-based. Transparent. Repeatable.

01

Scope

Agree objectives, criteria, sites, systems and stakeholders.

02

Collect

Interviews, document review, technical sampling and site walk-throughs.

03

Evaluate

Test controls against criteria and rate each finding by risk.

04

Report

Executive summary, detailed findings, evidence and recommendations.

05

Follow up

Corrective-action tracking and verification to closure.

Findings to closure

Turn every finding into an assigned task.

Clients can receive findings directly into Xploit GRC's findings register and task board, with owners, priorities and deadlines, so remediation is visible to everyone and nothing gets lost after the audit.

  • Central findings register for audit, assessment and vendor findings
  • Corrective actions with due dates and approval
  • Progress tracking and accountability on the dashboard
