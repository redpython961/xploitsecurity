Why awareness matters

Attackers target people first.

Humans remain the most vulnerable link in an organization's defense. Email phishing continues to be the most effective attack method, and the root cause is a lack of knowledge and defensive skills, not a technology flaw.

74%of breaches involved a human element, including phishing clicks, weak or stolen passwords and misconfigured systems.Verizon DBIR 2023
41%of cybersecurity incidents originated from phishing, the most common attack method.IBM Security X-Force 2023
41%of organizations say lack of time and resources is the biggest barrier to effective awareness training.SANS Security Awareness 2024
89%of respondents rank social engineering as the top human-related cyber risk.SANS Security Awareness 2024

Customer story

From easy target to prepared workforce.

A large enterprise in a highly sensitive industry, with over 5,000 employees, had invested heavily in firewalls and intrusion prevention. A third-party penetration test found a different reality: using public information such as job postings and customer service data, testers crafted targeted phishing emails, and the greatest risk lay in the habits and awareness of people.
