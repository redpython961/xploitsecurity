Why a simulator

A risk-free place to get it right.

Your live operations can't be a training ground. The simulator replicates real operating environments, from water plants to power generation and distribution facilities, so your people can learn and test without touching production.

Staff training

Build hands-on OT skills on realistic systems, not slides.

System modification testing

Try changes in the simulator before they reach the plant.

Cyber threat preparation

Rehearse attacks and your response in a safe environment.

Efficiency optimization

Explore how your systems behave and where they can improve.

Industrial process simulation

If it can happen in your plant, we can simulate it.

We replicate the environment you depend on, then trigger the events you could never rehearse in real life, so your team sees the consequences and practices the response.

What we can replicate

  • Wastewater treatment plants
  • Power grids
  • City lights
  • Metro stations and trains
  • and many more

Risks we can simulate

  • Explosions

  • Blackouts
  • Flooding
  • and many more

Plus any cybersecurity risk scenario, tested on the same replica.

Architecture

Your plant's whole stack, in one simulator.

A miniature replica with Level 0 devices

The system links to Level 0 devices, including sensors and pumps, all interacting with a miniature replica of the plant. Your team sees physical cause and effect, for a highly authentic learning experience.

Authentic PLC integration

PLCs sit at the heart of the simulation, connected to the Level 0 devices. Practical training and testing scenarios closely mimic real-world industrial operations.
