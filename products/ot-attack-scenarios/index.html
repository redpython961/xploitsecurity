What it is

A practical learning environment for industrial cyber risk.

The OT Cyber Range turns industrial cybersecurity concepts into decisions you actually make, so the right reflexes are already there when a real incident starts.

Industrial cybersecurity scenarios

Realistic incidents set in control rooms, substations and plants, built around ICS and SCADA environments.

OT threat awareness

USB drops, rogue vendors, phishing, ransomware, logic manipulation and physical intrusion.

ICS & SCADA attack concepts

Understand how attacks move from the office LAN to PLCs and HMIs, and how to stop them.

Practical, decision-based learning

Make the call at each step and see the operational consequences of every choice.

Understand industrial cyber risk

Connect cyber events to production, safety and physical outcomes.

Built for OT people

Designed for OT engineers, operators, cybersecurity professionals and students.

Training units

Ten incidents. Ten decisions that matter.

UNIT-01 · REMOVABLE MEDIA8 MIN

The USB Stick in the Car Park

A found USB device, a curious operator, and one decision that can reach the PLC network.

UNIT-02 · REMOTE ACCESS9 MIN

The Vendor Who Called Twice

An urgent support call, remote access to a live PLC, and a caller who is not who he claims to be.

UNIT-03 · PATCH & CHANGE8 MIN

The Unpatched HMI

A critical vulnerability advisory lands mid-production. Patch now, patch later, or something smarter?

UNIT-04 · PHISHING8 MIN

Phishing the Control Room

An email 'from the SCADA vendor' arrives with an urgent firmware notice, crafted for people exactly like you.

UNIT-05 · NETWORK SECURITY9 MIN

The Cable That Crossed the Zone

A convenient network shortcut quietly connects the office LAN to the control network, until you find it.

UNIT-06 · INCIDENT RESPONSE10 MIN

Ransomware at the Plant

Screens lock across the site. The next thirty minutes decide whether production, and safety, survive the day.

UNIT-07 · CONTROL INTEGRITY9 MIN

PLC Logic Manipulation

A pump behaves strangely, the HMI shows normal, and the controller logic no longer matches the golden master.

UNIT-08 · ACCESS CONTROL8 MIN

Default Passwords Expose Critical Systems

Brand-new RTUs, factory credentials, and a contractor who plans to 'change them later'.

UNIT-09 · PHYSICAL SECURITY8 MIN

The Fake Maintenance Technician

Hi-vis vest, toolbox, confident smile, urgent job, and no appointment in the system.

UNIT-10 · REMOTE ACCESS9 MIN

Compromised Vendor Remote Access

The 03:00 session comes from your trusted vendor's real account, but their engineer is on leave.
