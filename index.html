Introducing Xploit Security

One security partner for the office and the plant floor.

Xploit Security is the cybersecurity services and technology arm of the Xploit family. We protect critical infrastructure, enterprise applications and government organizations across the region and beyond, with practitioners who have run real IT and OT security programs.

We combine hands-on consulting, from penetration testing to vCISO leadership, with software we build ourselves: SafeMind for awareness, Xploit GRC for governance, Xploit Firewall Security Analyzer for firewall audits, and the SCADA Simulator for industrial cyber training and testing.
