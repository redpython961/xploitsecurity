Who we are

Practitioners who build what they use.

Xploit Security is the cybersecurity consulting, services and software business of the Xploit family. Our services are innovative, proactive and trusted, designed to strengthen digital resilience and compliance in an evolving threat landscape.

We work across both worlds most providers treat separately: enterprise IT, where data, users and cloud workloads live, and operational technology, where PLCs, SCADA and physical processes keep utilities and industry running. That converged view shapes everything we do, from a penetration test to a board-level risk report.

Alongside consulting, we develop our own software, SafeMind, Xploit GRC, Xploit Firewall Security Analyzer and the SCADA Simulator, because we wanted tools that remove work instead of creating it.

Our mission

To help organizations identify cyber risk early, strengthen their defenses and build lasting cyber resilience across IT and OT, with practical services and platforms that make security part of everyday operations.

Our vision

A world where enterprises and critical infrastructure operators can innovate with confidence because their people, systems and processes are secure by design, and trust is earned and proven.
